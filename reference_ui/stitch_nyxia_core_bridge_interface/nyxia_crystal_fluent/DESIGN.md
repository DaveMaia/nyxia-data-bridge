## Brand & Style

This design system blends the structural discipline of Windows 11 Fluent Design (Mica and Acrylic materials) with precision enterprise systems architecture. Designed for deep-tech neural bridge interfaces, mission-critical cognitive monitoring, and advanced systems routing, the UI communicates computational authority, calm transparency, and optical precision.

The aesthetic balances ethereal, frosted-glass layering with razor-sharp slate geometry. It eschews generic high-contrast neon glass tropes in favor of deliberate, tactile optical depth: ambient sub-surface illumination, finely honed border refraction, and calm structural restraint. The interface feels weightless yet grounded—engineered for operators who demand hours of focused data orchestration without visual fatigue.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural hierarchy rooted in frosted crystalline substrates and authoritative neural accents:

- **Surface System**: The foundation layer uses translucent Ice Gray (`#F1F5F9` at 0.94 alpha) backed by synthetic diffusion blurs. Floating surface planes leverage Pure Acrylic White (`#FFFFFF`) with 75%–90% opacity, while recessed actionable tracks and input wells sit at `#F8FAFC`. Polished perimeter lines utilize calibrated Slate strokes (`#CBD5E1` and translucent white `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`).
- **Core Primaries**: Deep Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`, hovering at `#1D4ED8`) commands direct action pathways, operational toggles, active routing nodes, and crisp focus rings.
- **Tech Intelligence**: Indigo / Violet (`#6366F1`) combined with light tinted cognitive backdrops (`#EDE9FE`) provides structural semantics for AI compute states, model versions, and telemetry pills.
- **Neural Signature Accent**: Bronze / Terracotta (`#C2410C` and `#B45309`) functions as the hardware-level bridge accent for specialized anchors, neural engine telemetry, and structural identity badges.
- **System States**:
  - *Success / Safe*: Emerald Green (`#059669`) with luminous wash (`#ECFDF5`) and perimeter edge (`#A7F3D0`).
  - *Warning / Throttle*: Warm Amber (`#D97706`) with tinted field (`#FEF3C7`) and border (`#FDE68A`).
  - *Danger / Interruption*: Soft Crimson (`#DC2626`) with soft alert background (`#FEE2E2`).
- **Typography Tones**: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) commands high contrast (95%) for titles and critical values, balanced by Medium Slate (`#64748B`) for metadata, units, and static labels.

## Typography

The typographic pairing relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural geometry in display headlines and section titles, transitioning to **Inter** for dense numerical tables, bridge logs, and functional interface components.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces subtle sculpted curves reminiscent of modern industrial hardware interfaces, mitigating eye fatigue while projecting human-engineered authority.
- **Inter** handles data-dense parameters, metrics, system labels, and form values. Its neutral metric rhythm guarantees pixel-perfect optical alignment alongside monospaced telemetry readouts and status badges.
- All numbers within bridge status tables, memory metrics, and latency registers must render with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column analytical grid with fixed outer gutters and fluid inner columns, maximizing vertical density while preserving generous breathing room around focal inspection cards.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content spans up to a max width of 1600px.
  - `Tablet (768px – 1279px)`: 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Navigation shifts to a translucent compact rail.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) canvas margins. Sidebars collapse into sliding glass sheets.
- **Rhythm & Grid Alignment**: Spacing tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) govern component-internal padding and element groupings. High-density metrics cards use `space-md` (16px) internal padding, whereas dominant overview cards utilize `space-lg` (24px) to retain structural lightness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows the physical metaphor of multi-tiered crystalline planes illuminated by a soft, directional light source (top-center ambient emission):

1. **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Ice Gray translucent tint (`#F1F5F9` at 94% opacity) layered over subtle ambient radial glows. Applied with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`.
2. **Layer 1 (Recessed Inputs & Wells)**: Inset frosted plane (`#F8FAFC`) with an ultra-light inner shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`) and a hairline edge (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
3. **Layer 2 (Floating Glass Cards & Panels)**: Pure White (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`), backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered by a 1px dual-luminance stroke: top and left borders highlighted with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, bottom and right borders anchored with `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Layer 3 (Overlays, Modals, Popovers)**: High-luminance frosted acrylic (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(190%)`, encased in crisp `1px solid rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.9)`. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14), 0 0 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework uses continuous curvature (squircle approximation) to maintain the organic fluidity of physical cast glass:

- **Large Containment (Cards, Floating Modals, Bridge Panels)**: `rounded-2xl` (1.25rem to 1.5rem / 20–24px).
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Tiles)**: `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to match Fluent acrylic standard touch and click targets.
- **Pills & Status Indicators**: Full pill curvature (`9999px`) for telemetry indicators, runtime states, version labels, and chip toggles.
- **Inner Corner Rule**: Nested child components maintain proportional concentricity (outer radius minus padding equals inner radius), eliminating optical distortion inside frosted frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`) solid fill, white text, 12px border radius, subtle top-edge inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Hover transitions to `#1D4ED8` with a diffused elevation glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)`). Active state applies subtle scale compression (0.98).
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`), Slate border (`1px solid #CBD5E1`), Deep Slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with `#94A3B8` border.
- **Tertiary / Bridge Action**: Warm Terracotta text (`#C2410C`) on transparent background with hover fill of `#FFF7ED` and border tint of `#FFEDD5`.

### Input Fields
- Recessed surface at `#F8FAFC`, 12px radius, surrounded by a calibrated slate border (`1px solid #CBD5E1`).
- Focus state activates an instantaneous crisp halo: border switches to Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`) accompanied by `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text calibrated at `#94A3B8`.

### Floating Cards & Panels
- Constructed from Pure Acrylic White (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, enclosed in a 1px boundary (`#CBD5E1`).
- Padding: Standard analytical card uses `20px` or `24px` (`space-lg`).
- Optional header accent: 1px linear gradient separator running from transparent to `#E2E8F0` to transparent.

### Chips & Version Pills
- **Cognitive / Model Version Pill**: Indigo background wash (`#EDE9FE`), text in `#6366F1`, full pill radius (`9999px`), 1px perimeter in `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`.
- **Telemetry Chips**: Height 24px, padding 2px 10px, typography `label-sm`, monospace numerical values.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18px × 18px with 5px radius for checkboxes; 18px round for radios.
- Unchecked: Frosted white base with 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- Checked: Deep Cobalt Blue fill with crisp optical white checkmark icon or radial center dot; soft drop glow of `0 2px 6px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.3)`.

### Lists & Bridge Telemetry Tables
- Row striping is discarded in favor of floating hover planes: rows transition to `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.6)` on hover with 8px radius corners.
- Dividers between records use subtle translucent hairlines (`rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.4)`).
- Status columns integrate glowing 6px status spheres (Emerald `#059669` for sync safe, Amber `#D97706` for queuing, Crimson `#DC2626` for dropouts).
